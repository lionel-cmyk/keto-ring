Privacy Policy

Cal & Carb Counter · Last updated July 15, 2026
Cal & Carb Counter collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

Your food log stays on your device

Everything you log — foods, servings, your daily goal — is stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Food database lookups

When you search for a packaged food or look up a barcode, the app sends only that search text or barcode number to Open Food Facts, a non-profit open food database, to retrieve nutrition facts. These requests contain no personal information and are not linked to you. They are subject to the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

What we don't do

We don't collect names, emails, health data, or identifiers. We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs. We don't sell data — we never have any to sell.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new date.
